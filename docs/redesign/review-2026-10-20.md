# Review of the redesign, 2026-10-20 (W7)

**What was reviewed:** `main` at `ac02b25` (W1–W6a merged), built with `npm run wasm`, served by Vite on port 5190 with the exchange rates mocked as in `tests/fixtures.ts`.
- **The core's words:** every situation, and every option of every decision with the decisions that follow it (84 paths), dumped from `answer` in Node under the page's starting settings. "Apartment" and "30-year saving" were also run with the bank set to Hapoalim, as §2.5 does.
- **The page:** Playwright at desktop 1280×800, Galaxy S24 and iPhone SE (3rd gen), taking each situation's first screen and a fully decided path ("can wait" on the place type, the first option everywhere else).
- **The keyboard:** a decision, then a chart row.

- **N16:** the where-to-buy note reads "ה‑4 האלה נבדלים זה מזה בפחות מ‑₪149". But IBI is ₪400 below Excellence; ₪149 is the spread of three.

  - **Fix:** have the core compute the tie group and its spread from the same set, as the highest minus the lowest, and have the note name the group it measured.

## Where Part 1 strains (principle 18), with proposed principles

**S1. A stand-in for the visitor's own fact.**
- **The case:** "לפי הוצאות של ₪8,000 בחודש, הערכה: כתבו את שלכם" follows §2.5, but it moves ₪24,000 and "about 4 years" before the visitor has said anything. Principles 5 and 6 say not to guess what only the visitor knows; §2.5 asks for exactly this guess.
- **Proposed:** "When a figure depends on a fact only the visitor knows, ask for it as a field at the point it decides, and show the consequence per unit until it's typed ('כל ₪1,000 של הוצאות חודשיות = חודשיים של הפקדות'), never a number the site picked."

**S2. The historical worst as a "version" (§2.3) against the history sentence (principle 16).**
- **The case:** the code runs the worst stretch after fees, tax and inflation, as §2.3 asks. The page still reports the past in dollars before fees, as principle 16's example wording does. The two disagree on the same row, which is the contradiction principle 11 forbids.
- **Proposed:** "Every statement on the answer about the past is on the same basis as the figure it sits beside (after fees and tax, in today's money). The raw history goes to the learn card."

**S3. "Can the date move?" for retirement money.**
- **The case:** the place-type question assumes the money is taken on one day. Retirement money taken as a pension is taken over 20+ years, and the provident fund converts it on the visitor's terms. Asking "can you wait a year or two?" there fits the letter of §2.3 (a version flips the answer), but a visitor wouldn't recognise the question as theirs.
- **Proposed:** "A question names the fact that makes it a question in this situation. If the honest version of that fact doesn't apply (money drawn as a pension, money already invested), the question isn't asked; the site states its call and shows the risk beside it."

**S4. "Each number once" (13) against "each option shows its ₪" (4) and "the chart is the list of options" (§2.6).**
- **The case:** the redesign puts the same amount on the decision card, the chart row and the where-to-buy row by construction, so a page shows ₪19,925 five times.
- **Proposed:** "An option's amount lives in the chart row. The decision card shows what choosing it changes (the ₪ difference from the other option, and what it asks), and where-to-buy shows differences from the reference row."

**S5. The noise margin covers the arena, not only the ranking.**
- **The case:** the margin of §2.3 (₪1,000 or 1%) is used to call places a tie. It isn't used when the index's worst beats the safe side by ₪2,000 (X4), nor for the Account decision with a ₪725 gap (bonus).
- **Proposed:** "The noise margin applies to every comparison the answer makes: arena, way and place. A difference under it is never a verdict, and never a question."
