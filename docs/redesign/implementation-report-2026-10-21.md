## Calls for the owner

These are where Part 1 strained (principle 18). Each is followed for now, with a proposal in the log:

1. **A number shown three times (principle 13).** A stock row's amount sits beside its name, again on its "10% בשנה" mark, and again as the plan's amount. Proposal: no amount on the mark where the row's own amount stands beside it.
2. **The plan changes what's above the pressed button (21/22).** The last choice turns the card into the plan. Mitigation: the step being used is held still. Proposal: name this as an exception in principle 22, or keep the lines until the visitor scrolls.
3. **A horizon row's tap (20/22).** It's a scroll the visitor asks for, but the row doesn't look like a button, and it overwrites the visitor's date with no way back. Proposal: a visible "להציג ›", and a ↺ back to their date.
4. **"מתאים אם" with three options on a phone.** It stays behind "עוד", as the frame has it, since each row's "מה זה דורש" already tells the any‑day options apart.
5. **The gain takeaway** now compares fees without the fund's own cost, so it reads "פי 17" where the frame had "פי 2.6". This keeps it consistent with the bars.
6. **The "already invested" box** from the `InputsCard` frame was kept, though Part 4's text doesn't mention it. It's the field the "כבר יש לי תיק בבנק" example sets.
